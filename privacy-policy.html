Privacy Policy for Photo Date Fix Effective date: 23 September 2026 Photo Date Fix helps users review and update the date and time metadata stored in their photos. Photos and metadata Photo Date Fix accesses only the photos that you choose through the Android system picker or share directly with the app. The app reads photo metadata to detect dates and, only after you confirm an update, writes the selected date and time to the photo’s EXIF metadata. Local processing Photos and metadata are processed locally on your device. To display previews and detect dates, the app may create an app-private cached copy of a selected photo. Cached copies remain on the device until Android or the user clears the app cache. No collection or sharing Photo Date Fix does not create user accounts. It does not upload, sell, share, or transmit your photos or photo metadata to a server or third party. Your choices You choose which photos to provide to the app. You can stop using the app at any time and remove cached data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. Contact For privacy questions, contact: contact@adeelbashir.com Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new effective date.
